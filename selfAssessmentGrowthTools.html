Self-Assessment & Growth Tools

Check your self-learning discipline & track your growth

This page is designed to help you evaluate your self-learning habits and use simple growth tools to stay disciplined over time.

By answering the questions below, you can:
  1. understand how disciplined you are in your self-learning routine
  2. plan a weekly study schedule for self-learning
  3. set a clear self-learning goal and track your progress step by step

1. What motivates you the most to practice self-learning?

A) Curiosity and passion for knowledge (1 point)
B) Improving professional skills (2 points)
C) Academic requirements (1 point)
D) Self-discipline and personal achievement (3 points)
